# Projection route decision
Route: **stylized (procedural materials), no pixel projection**.
Reason: the reference is a flat sticker with baked toon shading and outline; de-lighting and projection would bake 2D
conventions onto the mesh. Identity is carried by silhouette, proportions, flat colour regions (cheeks, belly, mouth,
iris gradient) and star catchlights, all of which are representable as vertex-region colour and small geometry.
Per-region confidence: face 0.9, body gradient 0.9, arms/hands 0.8, feet 0.8, rear/depth 0.6 (inferred).
